### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a third-year student majoring in Computer Engineering at the University of Information Technology. With a strong passion for the semiconductor industry and the knowledge I have gained about the ASIC design flow, I aspire to become a Physical Design engineer in the future.

I am particularly interested in the physical layout implementation of chips, where requirements such as Performance, Power, and Area must be carefully optimized to meet design specifications. Therefore, I continuously strive to deepen my knowledge in Physical Design in particular and integrated circuit design in general. I actively participate in laboratory work and academic projects to prepare myself for a career in the semiconductor industry.
